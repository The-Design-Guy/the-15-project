# ABOUT THE 15 PROJECT

## I HAD 15 MINUTES. SO I BUILT SOMETHING.

I'm a dad, a husband, a designer, and someone who's spent years building things for other people.

Websites. Brands. Digital products. You name it.

But somewhere between work, family, responsibilities, and the never-ending list of things that need to get done, I realized something.

**I'd gotten pretty good at taking care of everything except myself.**

Sound familiar?

I wasn't looking to become a professional athlete. I wasn't trying to completely reinvent my life.

I just wanted to feel a little more like myself again.

And I knew one thing for sure: I wasn't going to suddenly find two free hours every day to make that happen.

But 15 minutes?

**==I could find 15 minutes.==**

---

## FROM AN IDEA TO AN APP IN LESS THAN 48 HOURS.

So I started building.

Not for a client. Not for a deadline. Not because someone asked me to.

For myself.

I wanted something simple. Something that understood that life is busy, motivation comes and goes, and sometimes just getting started is the hardest part.

Short workouts. Realistic goals. A way to track progress. A little motivation.

And no pressure to become a completely different person overnight.

Less than 48 hours after the idea first took shape, I had a working application.

Was it perfect? Of course not.

But it existed.

And that felt pretty damn good.

**Sometimes the biggest step isn't the workout. ==It's deciding you're worth those 15 minutes.==**

---

## WHY I'M SHARING IT.

At first, THE 15 PROJECT was just for me.

But then I started thinking about how many people must feel exactly the same way.

Parents who are always putting everyone else first.

People who used to be active but somehow stopped.

People who look in the mirror and think, "When did I stop making time for myself?"

People who want to change something, but don't know where to start.

I figured that if I needed something like this, maybe someone else did too.

So I decided to share it.

Not because I have all the answers.

And definitely not because I'm a fitness expert.

**I'm simply someone trying to do a little better, one day at a time.**

---

## IT'S NOT REALLY ABOUT FITNESS.

Well, okay. It is a little about fitness.

But to me, it's about something bigger.

It's about keeping a small promise to yourself.

It's about creating a little space in your day that belongs to you.

It's about looking back after a few weeks and realizing that you've actually been showing up.

And maybe, somewhere along the way, recognizing that person in the mirror again.

That's why THE 15 PROJECT exists.

Not to change your entire life in 15 minutes.

**Just to remind you that you're still part of it.**
