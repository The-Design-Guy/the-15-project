# ABOUT THE 15 PROJECT

## I ONLY HAVE 15 MINUTES TO SPARE EACH DAY. ==SO I BUILT SOMETHING.==

I'm a dad, a husband, and a designer who's spent years building things for other people.

Somewhere along the way, I forgot to make time for myself.

I wanted to get stronger, feel better, and have the energy to keep up with my daughter. But finding an hour to exercise felt impossible.

**==15 minutes? I could do that.==**

---

## FROM AN IDEA TO AN APP.

So I built THE 15 PROJECT.

Short workouts. Realistic goals. A little motivation. No pressure to change your entire life overnight.

I built it for myself, but figured I couldn't be the only one who needed something like this.

**Sometimes the hardest part isn't the workout. ==It's deciding to start.==**
